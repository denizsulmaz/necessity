Data-Driven Campaigns

The story is already
inside your data.

We work with your datasets to find the narrative — patterns, outliers, changes over time — and build campaigns around what the data actually says, not what you assume it says.

  • Exploratory data analysis to find the story
  • Creative concept built around the evidence
  • Visual, motion and copy direction

Sonic Identity

Data you can hear.

We translate datasets into sound — and build brand audio identities that are mathematically derived from your organisation's data. Every note has a source. Every tone has a meaning.

  • Data sonification — turning numbers into music
  • Brand audio identity systems
  • Sonic logos derived from your data
  • Works for events, films, campaigns and products
Installations

Data as physical environment.

We design data-driven installations for cultural venues, corporate spaces, exhibitions, and live events — where the dataset becomes something audiences can walk through, touch, and experience in real time.

  • Responsive to live data feeds
  • Physical and hybrid (screen-based) formats
  • On-site support for installation and launch

How We Work

From data to
audience experience.

Marketing work begins with the data — not the brief. We find what the data wants to say before we decide how to say it.

Phase 01

Data Discovery

We explore your dataset to find the stories, patterns, and unexpected angles — the raw material for the campaign or experience.

Phase 02

Creative Direction

We propose a creative direction: the concept, medium, tone, and format. We present options — not a single answer — and align on direction before building.

Phase 03

Prototype & Refine

We build a working prototype of the experience — enough to feel real, test, and iterate on before committing to full production.

Phase 04

Production & Launch

Full production of the final deliverable — with support through launch, installation, or publication. We remain available through the live period.
