Common Questions

Straight answers

Send a message

Do you charge day rates?

Never. We price around scope, not time. You know the total cost before we start — no surprises if something takes longer than expected.

How quickly can you start?

Most projects begin within two weeks of sign-off. Productised packages can start within one week. Retainers typically start the following calendar month.

Can I try a small project before committing to a retainer?

Yes — and we'd encourage it. Many of our longest retainer relationships began as a single data audit or prototype. It's the best way to see how we work.

What if my data is sensitive or confidential?

We treat all client data as confidential by default. NDAs available on request. We never use client data for training AI models, ever.

Do you work with organisations outside the UK?

Yes. We work globally and are remote-first. Pricing is in GBP but we invoice in other currencies. Time zones have never been an obstacle.

What if my project doesn't fit any model?

Tell us about it. Some of our most interesting work comes from briefs that don't fit a standard category. If we can help, we'll say how. If we can't, we'll say so.
