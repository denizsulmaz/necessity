How We Work

From raw data to
published system.

Reporting projects follow a clear sequence — we never design before we understand the data. Accuracy is the foundation; design is what makes it usable.

Phase 01

Data Review

We examine your data — its structure, quality, gaps, and update frequency. We identify what can be reliably communicated and what can't.

Phase 02

Audience Mapping

Who reads this? What decisions do they need to make? What level of detail is useful versus overwhelming? Design follows from answers here.

Phase 03

Design & Build

We design the visual system and build the reporting infrastructure — data connections, update logic, interaction patterns, and responsive layouts.

Phase 04

Delivery & Handover

We deliver the system and provide documentation for your team. Ongoing maintenance and updates available through a retainer arrangement.

Public Reports

Reports that people actually read.

Data-heavy publications don't have to be inaccessible. We design annual reports, research documents, and data publications that balance rigour with clarity — suitable for wide audiences.

  • PDF, web or both — designed for the medium

Interactive Tools

Let your audience explore the data themselves.

Some data is too complex for a single view. Interactive tools give audiences control — to filter, compare, and find the answer most relevant to them.

  • Filter, drill-down, and comparative views
  • Embeds into existing websites or standalone
  • Accessible and mobile-optimised
  • Can connect to live data sources

What Sets Our Reporting Apart

Design-forward from
the first data point.

⬡

Accuracy first, always

We never design around incomplete or misleading data. Our process begins with a data audit — if it can't be shown accurately, we won't show it.

◎

Built for real audiences

We design for the people who will actually read the work — not a hypothetical expert user. Accessibility and clarity are requirements, not bonuses.

▲

Design-forward thinking

Reporting doesn't have to look like reporting. Our work is visually distinguished, brand-coherent, and built to communicate — not just display.

◈

Systems, not one-offs

We build with reuse in mind. The visual system, data pipeline, and component library are all documented so your team can update and extend independently.

Who This Is For

Reporting work is right
for these organisations.

Internal Teams

You need your team to understand and act on data faster.

Dashboards and monitoring systems for product, operations, research, or leadership teams. Designed around actual workflows, not generic BI templates.

Examples: Research organisations, operational teams, data-driven businesses

Public Communication

You publish data and want it to reach a wider audience effectively.

Annual reports, research publications, and public data portals designed to communicate clearly to non-specialist audiences without sacrificing depth.

Examples: NGOs, cultural institutions, government bodies, research centres

Legacy Systems

Your current reporting tools no longer fit how you work.

You're using spreadsheets, outdated dashboards, or static PDFs that take too long to produce and don't reflect the complexity of your organisation.

Examples: Established brands, institutional organisations, scaling teams

Multiple Audiences

Different stakeholders need to see the same data differently.

Executive summaries, team-level dashboards, and public-facing reports often need to draw from the same source but present very different views.

Examples: Multi-stakeholder organisations, investor-facing teams, grant-funded bodies
