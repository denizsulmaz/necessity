Phase 01 — Brief

Understanding data can also mean opening it up to poetry.

As part of Mango's 40th anniversary celebration, we were invited to build a data-driven piece of art which would celebrate the entire team at Mango and their vital importance as the driving force behind the company. We wanted to highlight the different values of a diverse team, and through visual, aesthetic and tangible means, craft an experience that would be inspiring and accessible to everyone at Mango.

Phase 02 — Survey

A team-wide poll to surface the real values.

We kicked off by conducting a survey across all of Mango's teams to discover the main values guiding them every day. Each question invited them to think about their personal approach — for instance, when considering the value of "Caring," we explored whether they prioritize listening or offering adviceThis question mapped to the Midday wind — soft & warm when supporting others. Through these questions, we introduced the parallelism between winds and values.

Phase 03 — Design

Data shaped the sculpture's form.

Once we received all the data, we processed and parametrized the numerical results by representing them as bar charts — then used those charts to trace and design the sculpture's data-based component. The team's responses almost literally determined the piece's form and measurements. Product designer Gennis Sennen helped decide on materiality and contributed to the overall structure.

Phase 04 — Craft

Three artisans. One modern creation.

We made the conscious choice of building a modern artisanal creation — evoking expertise rooted in tradition, yet still new and creative. We worked with local glass artist Ferran Collado to craft the glass sculpture, alongside artisans in metalwork and stonecarving. To connect with retail workers across locations, we also created five smaller-scale replicas of the sculpture.

Phase 05 — Film

Documenting the creative journey.

The final component was a short film explaining the journey — from conceptualization to production — highlighting the artisanal aspect. Narrated by a speaking voice identified as the wind itself, the video passes through a dozen places around Catalunya, capturing the Mediterranean essence. Made for the Mango team specifically, it's a quiet reflection on the creative process as a meeting point between enduring practices and new ideas.
