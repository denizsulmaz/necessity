How We Work

A structured process
for complex problems.

Strategy is not a single output — it's a sequence. We move through defined phases, each building on the last, so nothing is built on untested assumptions.

Phase 01

Discovery & Audit

We map your existing data landscape, tools, and workflows. Understanding what you have before imagining what's possible.

Phase 02

Opportunity Analysis

We identify where data, AI or algorithms could create genuine value — ranked by feasibility, impact, and strategic fit.

Phase 03

Concept & Validation

Key ideas are developed into testable concepts. We prototype where needed to validate assumptions before committing resources.

Phase 04

Roadmap Delivery

A clear, executable strategy document your team can act on — with defined next steps, timelines, and decision points.

Who This Is For

Strategy work fits two kinds
of organisations.

Modernising

You have data but no clear direction for how to use it.

Your current tools and workflows no longer reflect what your organisation needs. You want to understand what's possible before committing to a build.

Examples: Cultural institutions, research organisations, established brands

Building New

You're ready to build something ambitious and need a technical partner to shape the idea.

You already work with data and technology, and you want to explore new ways to turn it into products, experiences or brand assets.

Examples: Technology companies, innovation teams, forward-thinking brands

Pitching

You need a prototype or proposal to secure internal buy-in or investor funding.

Strategy work often culminates in a stakeholder-ready deliverable. We regularly support teams preparing innovation proposals and funding applications.

Examples: Internal innovation teams, startups, NGOs

Exploring

You have a vague idea and need help understanding whether it's viable.

Many projects start here. We can sense-check the concept, assess technical feasibility, and help you decide whether to proceed — and how.

Examples: Any organisation curious about AI, data or algorithmic systems
