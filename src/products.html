How Products Are Built

From prototype to
production system.

Every product starts with a short discovery phase — not to gather requirements, but to understand what the product actually needs to do. We build in phases so nothing is committed to prematurely.

Phase 01 — Discovery

Define the problem before defining the product

We audit your data, understand your users, and map what the product actually needs to do. Many products change significantly at this stage — intentionally.

Phase 02 — Prototype

Build something real before committing to full production

A working prototype — not wireframes — tested against real data and real users. Fast, honest, and designed to surface what written specs can't predict.

Phase 03 — Production

Build the full system to production standard

Full engineering and design build — performant, accessible, and documented. Developed iteratively with checkpoints at defined milestones.

Phase 04 — Handover

Transfer ownership completely — no dependency required

Source code, documentation, deployment guides, and handover sessions. You leave with everything you need to own and run the product independently.
